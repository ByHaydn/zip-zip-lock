# Welcome to Zip Zip Lock! 🚀 (macOS Quick Start & User Guide)

Hey there! Thank you so much for choosing **Zip Zip Lock** to keep your files safe and organized. We built this app to give you peace of mind, combining strong privacy with a super simple, daily drag-and-drop experience.

Since your Mac runs on Apple's powerful **M-Series chips (M1, M2, M3, M4, M5)**, this app is fully optimized to run natively. This means it is blazing fast, uses almost zero battery, and works entirely offline.

Let’s get you set up in just a few quick steps!

---

## 🛡️ 1. How the Locking Modes Work (Read This First!)

We offer two different ways to lock your files, depending on what you need:

### A. Manual Password Mode (Simple & Daily)
*   **What it is:** You type your own password (like a word or numbers) to lock your files.
*   ⚠️ **Important Warning:** If you lock a file with a manual password and forget it, **Touch ID cannot open it later**. This is because that specific file was encrypted using your typed password. Please write down or remember your manual passwords!

### B. Touch ID Mode (Biometric Ease)
*   **What it is:** You lock your files with a simple click of the `Lock with Touch ID` button.
*   **Why it's great:** You don't have to type passwords. Just scan your fingerprint to open the file!
*   💡 **Your Safety Net:** If you ever forget your password or your fingerprint sensor acts up, you can easily unlock these biometric files using your **12-word recovery phrase**. 

---

## 🚫 2. Accidental Deletion Protection (System-Level Lock Alert)

We know how easy it is to accidentally delete important files. That's why once you lock a file into a `.zzl` archive:
*   Our app flags the file as locked at the system level (`chflags uchg`).
*   **This prevents accidental dragging to the Trash or overwriting in Finder.**
*   If you attempt to move the locked file to the Trash, macOS Finder will stop you and display a warning: *"This item is locked. Do you want to move it to the Trash anyway?"* This acts as a critical buffer to prevent accidental loss. 
*   *Please Note:* If you explicitly select "Continue" on the macOS warning and choose to empty the Trash, macOS Finder will delete the file as an administrative action. Always backup your critical `.zzl` files!

## 🚀 4. How to Install It on Your Mac

1.  Double-click the **`Zip-Zip-Lock_macOS.dmg`** file you downloaded.
2.  Drag the **`Zip Zip Lock`** icon directly into your **`Applications`** folder.
3.  **Opening it for the first time:**
    *   Go to your `Applications` folder in Finder.
    *   **Right-click** (or hold Control and click) `Zip Zip Lock` and choose **`Open`**.
    *   Click **`Open`** on the popup that appears. (macOS does this to confirm you trust independent developers. You only have to do this once!).

---

## 🔑 5. Setting Up Your 12-Word Recovery Phrase

Think of this like a backup key for your house.
1.  Open the app and scroll down to the **Recovery Phrase** section.
2.  Click **`Generate Words`**.
3.  Copy the 12 words that appear.
4.  **Write them down on a physical piece of paper** and store it somewhere safe (like a drawer).
5.  Click **`Save / Verify`** to register it on your Mac.

---

## 🔒 6. Locking Your First Files

1.  Drag and drop any files or folders directly into the **Lock File / Folder** zone in the app.
2.  Choose whether you are locking a folder or files. **You can select multiple files or multiple folders at the same time!**
3.  Type a manual password, or click the link to generate a secure recovery password.
4.  Click **`Lock with Password`** or **`Lock with Touch ID`**.
5.  Your files are safely locked, and Finder will automatically open to show you where your new secure `.zzl` file is located!

---

## 🔓 7. Unlocking Your Files

1.  Drag your locked `.zzl` files into the **Unlock** zone. (You can drag multiple locked files at once to unlock them in bulk!).
2.  Enter the password, or click **`Unlock with Touch ID`** and place your finger on your Mac's sensor.
3.  Click the unlock button.
4.  Your files will instantly return to their original format and highlight themselves in Finder!

If you ever need help or have questions, feel free to reach out to us. Happy locking! 🔒✨
